Privacy Policy for WER2 Tax & Tip Calculator

Effective date: October 2, 2026

WER2 Tax & Tip Calculator is a calculator application designed to help users calculate restaurant bills, sales tax, tips, and totals. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information the App Uses

Location information: If you grant location permission and use the location-based tax-rate feature, the app obtains your device location to help determine a suggested California sales tax rate. The app sends latitude and longitude to the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration (CDTFA) tax-rate service to request the applicable tax-rate information. The app does not intentionally retain your precise location as part of your saved calculator history.

If you do not grant location permission, or if a tax rate cannot be determined automatically, you can enter the tax rate manually.

Calculator information: Information you enter into the calculator, such as item descriptions, amounts, tax rate, and tip percentage, is used to perform calculations. Saved calculations and screenshots are stored locally on your device for the app's Save and History features.

2. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

WER2 Tax & Tip Calculator does not require an account or login and does not intentionally collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos, or payment-card information for the calculator's normal operation.

3. Third-Party Services

California Department of Tax and Fee Administration (CDTFA): When you use the location-based tax-rate feature, location coordinates are transmitted to the CDTFA tax-rate service so that the app can request a suggested tax rate. CDTFA's own privacy policy governs information handled by CDTFA.

PayPal: The app may open a PayPal webpage for users who choose to support the app. Payments are handled by PayPal, not by WER2 Tax & Tip Calculator.

Zelle: The app may display information for users who choose to support the app through Zelle. The app does not process or handle Zelle payments. Transactions are completed through the user's bank or financial institution.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Saved calculator records and screenshots created through the app's Save and History features are stored locally on your device. The app does not intentionally upload those saved calculator records to a WER2 server.

Location information used for the tax-rate lookup is not intentionally retained by the app as part of saved calculator records. Third-party services may have their own data-handling and retention practices; users should review the applicable third-party privacy policies.

5. Location Permission

You control whether the app can access your location through iOS permissions. You can deny location access and continue to use the calculator by entering the tax rate manually. You can change location permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary collection and storage of information. Communications to third-party services are made through standard internet connections. No method of electronic transmission or storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

WER2 Tax & Tip Calculator is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly request personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's data practices or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will include a revised effective date.
